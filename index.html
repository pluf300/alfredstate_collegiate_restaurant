Our Story

Our Story

A Century of Community

The Collegiate has been a beloved fixture in Alfred since 1924 — a place where neighbors become friends and every cup of coffee comes with good conversation.

For decades, the Ninos family poured their hearts into this corner of Main Street, building a legacy that became inseparable from the village itself. In 2025, Alfred State College took on the stewardship of that legacy, ensuring The Collegiate endures for generations to come.

Today, the restaurant serves a dual purpose: a welcoming spot for the community, and a hands-on training ground where Alfred State culinary students develop real-world skills in food preparation, business, and hospitality.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you take reservations?

The Collegiate is a walk-in restaurant — no reservations required. We welcome all guests on a first-come, first-served basis during regular hours.

Are you open during school breaks and summer?

Because The Collegiate serves as a hands-on training ground for Alfred State culinary students, our schedule follows the college academic calendar. Hours may be reduced or temporarily suspended during school breaks and summer. Check our Facebook or Instagram pages, or call ahead, for the most current schedule.

Is the food prepared by students?

Yes! Alfred State Culinary Arts students help prepare many of our daily menu items — including our rotating dessert specials. All food is made under the direct supervision of professional culinary instructors to ensure consistent quality.

Do you offer takeout?

Yes, takeout is available. Call us at (607) 247-5010 to place your order, or stop in during business hours and we’ll have it ready for you.

What kind of food do you serve?

We serve breakfast and lunch classics made fresh daily — eggs, pancakes, sandwiches, soups, and student-crafted desserts that change with each semester. The menu rotates regularly to reflect the current culinary curriculum.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions or allergies?

We do our best to work with common dietary needs. Please let your server know about any allergies or restrictions when you arrive and we’ll do everything we can to find a suitable option for you.

Is parking available?

Street parking is available along N. Main Street in downtown Alfred. Alfred State campus lots are also within easy walking distance of the restaurant.

Is the restaurant wheelchair accessible?

Yes, The Collegiate is wheelchair accessible. If you have specific accessibility needs, feel free to call us ahead of your visit so we can ensure a comfortable experience.

Can I book the space for a private event or group visit?

For group visits or private event inquiries, contact us at Collegiate@AlfredState.edu or call (607) 247-5010 and we’ll be happy to discuss options.

Can I pay with Alfred State meal swipes or Dining Dollars?

No — meal swipes and Dining Dollars are not accepted at The Collegiate. We accept cash and major credit or debit cards only.

Do you accept credit cards?

Yes, we accept major credit and debit cards as well as cash.
